Privacy Policy for NijiScan

Last updated: September 11, 2026

NijiScan ("the App", "we", "us") is developed and published by MGB Studio ("the Developer"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, and how it is used.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at nijiscan@gmail.com.

Summary (in plain language)

  • Your documents never leave your device. NijiScan does not require an account, does not upload your scans to any server, and has no cloud storage. Everything — scanning, cropping, enhancement, PDF creation, Notes, and any password/PIN you set — happens locally on your phone.
  • We don't run our own servers that see your content. The only network activity in the App comes from third-party services described below: Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (the one-time Pro purchase).
  • Uninstalling the App deletes everything stored inside it. This includes documents, Notes, and settings kept in the App's own storage. If you have separately used the App's "Save to Downloads" option for a PDF or image, that specific copy is saved to your device's public Downloads folder and is not deleted when you uninstall the App. Files you only shared (without also saving them to Downloads) are not guaranteed to persist — they exist only in a temporary location the App uses for that purpose, which is cleared on uninstall and may also be cleared automatically by your device at other times.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and have no access to:

  • The photos, scans, or documents you create or import into NijiScan. These items remain on your local device only, since we do not host or upload them to any external cloud or server.
  • The content of any Notes you create.
  • Any PIN, pattern, password, or security-question answer you set for Lock or for a password-protected exported PDF — stored only on your device, never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Your name, email, or any account information — NijiScan does not require sign-up or login.

3. Third-Party Services Used in the App

Google AdMob (advertising). NijiScan shows banner ads and optional rewarded video ads (used to unlock premium features temporarily) via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), IP address, and app interaction data to serve, optimize, and measure ads, and — where you have consented — to personalize them.

  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's own ad settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"). If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the App shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it's relevant, and you can revisit or change your choice at any time through the "Ad Privacy Options" entry in the App's menu.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchase). The one-time "Pro" purchase (ad removal + premium unlock) is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. NijiScan never sees or stores your payment details — Google Play handles that. We only receive confirmation a purchase was completed, tied to your Google Play account, so it can be restored on reinstall or a new device.

  • Google Play Terms of Service: play.google.com/about/play-terms

4. Data Shared With Third Parties

MGB Studio does not compile, store, transfer, or sell your data, and does not gather or track user information on our own servers — we don't operate any. The only data that leaves your device is the technical and advertising data described in Section 3, handled entirely by Google through its AdMob and Play Billing systems.

5. Data Security

  • In-app document lock (PIN or pattern) — a screen-level lock on individual documents. Not full-disk encryption.
  • Password-protected PDF export — standard PDF password protection, intended as a basic deterrent rather than enterprise-grade encryption for highly sensitive documents.

6. Children's Privacy

NijiScan is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access/Delete: Delete any document in-app at any time, or delete everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Ads personalization: Manage this through your device's own ad settings, and, if you're in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, through the "Ad Privacy Options" entry in the App's menu at any time.
  • Purchase records: Held by Google Play under your Google account; manage them via the Play Store app.

8. International Users

NijiScan is developed in India and primarily intended for users in India, but is available globally via Google Play. Users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland are shown an ad-consent form as required by local law, managed through Google's User Messaging Platform — see Section 3.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
